# 彼得兔的故事 / The Tale of Peter Rabbit

> 比阿特丽克斯·波特（Beatrix Potter）| 1902 年首次出版

---

**EN** Once upon a time there were four little Rabbits, and their names were—Flopsy, Mopsy, Cotton-tail, and Peter.

**中** 从前有四只小兔子，他们的名字分别是——弗洛普茜、莫普茜、棉尾巴，还有彼得。

**EN** They lived with their Mother in a sand-bank, underneath the root of a very big fir-tree.

**中** 他们和妈妈一起住在一棵大冷杉树根下的沙堤里。

---

**EN** 'Now, my dears,' said old Mrs. Rabbit one morning, 'you may go into the fields or down the lane, but don't go into Mr. McGregor's garden: your Father had an accident there; he was put in a pie by Mrs. McGregor.'

**中** "好了，亲爱的孩子们，"兔妈妈一天早上说，"你们可以去田野里玩，也可以去小路上走走，但千万别进麦格雷戈先生的菜园。你们的爸爸就是在那里出的事——他被麦格雷戈太太做成了馅饼。"

**EN** 'Now run along, and don't get into mischief. I am going out.'

**中** "好了，去吧，别捣乱。我要出门了。"

**EN** Then old Mrs. Rabbit took a basket and her umbrella, and went through the wood to the baker's. She bought a loaf of brown bread and five currant buns.

**中** 于是兔妈妈拿着篮子和雨伞，穿过树林去面包店了。她买了一条黑面包和五个葡萄干小圆面包。

---

**EN** Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail, who were good little bunnies, went down the lane to gather blackberries:

**中** 弗洛普茜、莫普茜和棉尾巴是乖孩子，她们沿着小路去摘黑莓了。

**EN** But Peter, who was very naughty, ran straight away to Mr. McGregor's garden, and squeezed under the gate!

**中** 但是彼得非常淘气，他径直跑向麦格雷戈先生的菜园，从大门底下钻了进去！

---

**EN** First he ate some lettuces and some French beans; and then he ate some radishes;

**中** 他先吃了一些生菜和四季豆，然后又吃了一些小萝卜。

**EN** And then, feeling rather sick, he went to look for some parsley.

**中** 吃完之后他觉得有点不舒服，想去找点西芹来吃。

---

**EN** But round the end of a cucumber frame, whom should he meet but Mr. McGregor!

**中** 但在一排黄瓜架的尽头，他迎面碰上了谁呢？正是麦格雷戈先生！

**EN** Mr. McGregor was on his hands and knees planting out young cabbages, but he jumped up and ran after Peter, waving a rake and calling out, 'Stop thief!'

**中** 麦格雷戈先生正跪在地上种卷心菜苗，但他一下子跳起来追彼得，挥舞着耙子大喊："抓小偷！"

---

**EN** Peter was most dreadfully frightened; he rushed all over the garden, for he had forgotten the way back to the gate.

**中** 彼得吓得要命，在菜园里乱跑，因为他已经忘了回大门的路。

**EN** He lost one of his shoes among the cabbages, and the other shoe amongst the potatoes.

**中** 他把一只鞋子丢在了卷心菜地里，另一只鞋子丢在了土豆地里。

**EN** After losing them, he ran on four legs and went faster, so that I think he might have got away altogether if he had not unfortunately run into a gooseberry net, and got caught by the large buttons on his jacket. It was a blue jacket with brass buttons, quite new.

**中** 丢了鞋子之后，他用四条腿跑，跑得更快了。我觉得他本来完全可以逃脱的，可惜他不巧撞进了一张醋栗网里，被外套上的大纽扣钩住了。那是一件蓝色外套，铜纽扣，还是崭新的呢。

---

**EN** Peter gave himself up for lost, and shed big tears; but his sobs were overheard by some friendly sparrows, who flew to him in great excitement, and implored him to exert himself.

**中** 彼得觉得自己完了，流下了大颗大颗的眼泪。但几只好心的小麻雀听到了他的哭泣声，它们兴奋地飞到他身边，恳求他再努力一把。

**EN** Mr. McGregor came up with a sieve, which he intended to pop upon the top of Peter; but Peter wriggled out just in time, leaving his jacket behind him.

**中** 麦格雷戈先生拿着一个筛子走过来，打算把彼得扣在下面。但彼得及时挣脱了，把外套留在了身后。

---

**EN** And rushed into the tool-shed, and jumped into a can. It would have been a beautiful thing to hide in, if it had not had so much water in it.

**中** 他冲进工具棚，跳进了一个水罐里。要不是罐子里有那么多水，这倒是个绝妙的藏身之处。

**EN** Mr. McGregor was quite sure that Peter was somewhere in the tool-shed, perhaps hidden underneath a flower-pot. He began to turn them over carefully, looking under each.

**中** 麦格雷戈先生确信彼得就在工具棚里，也许藏在哪个花盆底下。于是他开始一个个地仔细翻看。

**EN** Presently Peter sneezed—'Kertyschoo!' Mr. McGregor was after him in no time.

**中** 不一会儿，彼得打了一个喷嚏——"阿嚏！"麦格雷戈先生立刻追了过来。

---

**EN** And tried to put his foot upon Peter, who jumped out of a window, upsetting three plants. The window was too small for Mr. McGregor, and he was tired of running after Peter. He went back to his work.

**中** 他想踩住彼得，但彼得跳出了窗户，还撞翻了三个花盆。窗户太小了，麦格雷戈先生钻不过去，而且他也追彼得追累了，就回去干活了。

**EN** Peter sat down to rest; he was out of breath and trembling with fright, and he had not the least idea which way to go. Also he was very damp with sitting in that can.

**中** 彼得坐下来休息，他上气不接下气，吓得发抖，完全不知道该往哪儿走。而且刚才蹲在那个水罐里，浑身都湿透了。

**EN** After a time he began to wander about, going lippity—lippity—not very fast, and looking all round.

**中** 过了一会儿，他开始四处徘徊，一蹦一跳地，走得不太快，边走边东张西望。

---

**EN** He found a door in a wall; but it was locked, and there was no room for a fat little rabbit to squeeze underneath.

**中** 他发现墙上有一扇门，但是锁着，而且门缝太小，一只胖嘟嘟的小兔子根本钻不过去。

**EN** An old mouse was running in and out over the stone doorstep, carrying peas and beans to her family in the wood. Peter asked her the way to the gate, but she had such a large pea in her mouth that she could not answer. She only shook her head at him. Peter began to cry.

**中** 一只老鼠在石头门阶上跑来跑去，给住在树林里的家人搬运豌豆和豆子。彼得向她问去大门的路，可她嘴里塞着一颗好大的豌豆，根本没法回答，只能对他摇了摇头。彼得又哭了起来。

---

**EN** Then he tried to find his way straight across the garden, but he became more and more puzzled. Presently, he came to a pond where Mr. McGregor filled his water-cans. A white cat was staring at some gold-fish, she sat very, very still, but now and then the tip of her tail twitched as if it were alive. Peter thought it best to go away without speaking to her; he had heard about cats from his cousin, little Benjamin Bunny.

**中** 然后他想径直穿过菜园找路，但越走越迷糊。不一会儿，他来到一个池塘边——麦格雷戈先生就是在这里给水罐装水的。一只白猫正盯着几条金鱼看，她坐得非常非常安静，但时不时地，她的尾巴尖会抽动一下，好像活的一样。彼得觉得最好别跟她说话，赶紧走开——他从他的堂兄小兔本杰明那里听说过猫的厉害。

---

**EN** He went back towards the tool-shed, but suddenly, quite close to him, he heard the noise of a hoe—scr-r-ritch, scratch, scratch, scritch. Peter scuttered underneath the bushes.

**中** 他往回朝工具棚走去，但突然，他听到很近的地方传来锄头的声音——唰——唰——唰——唰。彼得赶紧窜到灌木丛下面。

**EN** But presently, as nothing happened, he came out, and climbed upon a wheelbarrow and peeped over. The first thing he saw was Mr. McGregor hoeing onions. His back was turned towards Peter, and beyond him was the gate!

**中** 过了一会儿，没有什么动静，他就出来了，爬上一辆独轮车，偷偷往那边看。他第一眼看到的就是麦格雷戈先生在锄洋葱。他的背对着彼得，而他的后面就是大门！

**EN** Peter got down very quietly off the wheelbarrow; and started running as fast as he could go, along a straight walk behind some black-currant bushes.

**中** 彼得非常轻巧地从独轮车上跳下来，沿着黑醋栗丛后面的一条直道，拼命跑了起来。

**EN** Mr. McGregor caught sight of him at the corner, but Peter did not care. He slipped underneath the gate, and was safe at last in the wood outside the garden.

**中** 麦格雷戈先生在拐角处看到了他，但彼得已经不在乎了。他从大门底下钻了出去，终于安全了——他回到了菜园外面的树林里。

---

**EN** Mr. McGregor hung up the little jacket and the shoes for a scare-crow to frighten the blackbirds.

**中** 麦格雷戈先生把那件小外套和那双鞋子挂起来做了一个稻草人，用来吓唬乌鸫。

**EN** Peter never stopped running or looked behind him till he got home to the big fir-tree.

**中** 彼得一口气跑回了大冷杉树下的家，一路上没有停下，也没有回头。

**EN** He was so tired that he flopped down upon the nice soft sand on the floor of the rabbit-hole and shut his eyes. His mother was busy cooking; she wondered what he had done with his clothes. It was the second little jacket and pair of shoes that Peter had lost in a fortnight!

**中** 他累坏了，扑通一声倒在兔子洞里柔软的沙地上，闭上了眼睛。妈妈正忙着做饭，她纳闷彼得又把衣服弄到哪里去了。这已经是彼得两周之内丢的第二件小外套和第二双鞋了！

---

**EN** I am sorry to say that Peter was not very well during the evening.

**中** 很遗憾，到了晚上彼得就不太舒服了。

**EN** His mother put him to bed, and made some camomile tea; and she gave a dose of it to Peter!

**中** 妈妈让他上了床，煮了一些甘菊茶，还喂彼得喝了一勺！

**EN** 'One table-spoonful to be taken at bed-time.'

**中** "睡前服用一汤匙。"

---

**EN** But Flopsy, Mopsy, and Cotton-tail had bread and milk and blackberries for supper.

**中** 而弗洛普茜、莫普茜和棉尾巴呢，她们晚餐吃的是面包、牛奶和黑莓。

**EN** THE END

**中** 全文完
